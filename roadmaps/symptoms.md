# Symptom Logger — roadmap

Status: idea (2026-11-16). Floated alongside the food app: a quick daily log of
symptoms (e.g. disability-related), kept in the same user-controlled record as the
other apps so the pre-session brief can weigh symptoms, food and training together.

## First version

- [ ] Decide what a day's entry holds (which symptoms, scale, free note)
- [ ] Quick daily entry screen, phone-first
- [ ] Local storage and backup to the user's own drive, per `STANDARDS.md`
- [ ] Export the log in a shape the shared record and the pre-session brief can read

## Later

- [ ] One central record shared with Ironlog and Food
- [ ] Trends view next to training load
