# Food Bank Stock — roadmap

**Status:** idea · single user (Aaron), on his phone

Stock only — no recipient or client details, ever. Small operation, about 20 people a session.
Two areas: outside (short-dated donations and the coffee area) and the backroom (boxes of
basics being prepped, frozen chicken, toiletries). Stock is tracked by shelf or crate.

## Phase 1
- [ ] Locations: area → shelf or crate
- [ ] Items with quantity, location and best-before date
- [ ] Quick plus / minus on a session day
- [ ] Low-stock list against a minimum per item
- [ ] Short-dated list: what to put out first
- [ ] Export / import in the shared backup envelope; CSV for the coordinators
- [ ] Repo, Pages, PWA shell

## Phase 2
- [ ] Barcode scan to find or add an item (shares code with Food's scanner)
- [ ] Box templates: "basics box" = list of items, deducted in one tap
- [ ] Freezer log for the frozen chicken (date in, date out)
